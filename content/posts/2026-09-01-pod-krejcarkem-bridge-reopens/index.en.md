The Technical Road Administration (TSK) has finished the reconstruction of the road bridge in Pod
Krejcárkem street, the link between Ohrada and Palmovka, timing completion to the last day of the
school holidays. Work began in April and traffic was restricted in phases throughout, Zdopravy.cz
reported on 31 August.

## What was replaced

The list is the standard inventory of a bridge that has reached the end of a service cycle rather
than the end of its life: the bridge deck was repaired, the waterproofing replaced, along with the
pavement layers, the expansion joints and the bearings. Drainage and safety features were restored.
TSK says the work has extended the bridge's life "for many more years".

Bearings and expansion joints are the tell. They are the parts that let a bridge move — thermally,
under load — and replacing both at once means the structure was overdue rather than damaged. Renewing
the waterproofing and the surfacing in the same season is what stops the process starting again from
the top down.

## The August shutdown

For most of the job the bridge stayed partly open. In August the scope widened: repairs to the
carriageway of Pod Krejcárkem street itself were added, together with alterations to the Ohrada
junction and work on the tram track running under the bridge. At that point the bridge was closed
completely, with one-way traffic held in a single lane towards the junction of Pod Plynojemem and
Novovysočanská.

Bundling the junction, the street surface and the tram track into the window when the bridge was
already shut is the sensible way to do it, and it is the reason the closure tightened rather than
eased in its final month.

## Buses go back

The closure moved bus routes and stops, and those now revert. In the direction of Jižní Město —
respectively Točná and Smíchovské nádraží — services had been running as far as the K Žižkovu stop
and then continuing through K Žižkovu, Spojovací and Hartigova streets. From the reopening they
follow their normal lines again.

The detour is a fair measure of what the bridge does. Pod Krejcárkem carries the direct connection
between Ohrada and Palmovka; without it, buses had to be threaded through three named streets on the
Žižkov side to reach the same places. Restoring it before the school year starts, rather than a
fortnight into September, matters more for the morning peak than the engineering does.
