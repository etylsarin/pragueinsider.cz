From 13 December, the trains that climb out of Smíchov over the Prague Semmering towards Hostivice
will be quicker, newer and, at peak, twice as frequent from Hostivice. Line S65, which runs from
Prague through Hostivice to Rudná u Prahy, will be worked by modernised Stadler RS1 railcars instead
of the class 810 units that have served it for decades, and its journey time will fall by more than
ten minutes against the current timetable. Zdopravy.cz reported the changes on 21 November from
current information published by ROPID, the organiser of Prague's integrated transport system.

## What the station rebuild gives back

The time saving is not the new trains. It is the reconstruction of Praha-Smíchov station.

S65 trains used to have a separate northern platform of their own. It was abolished in the autumn
of 2023 to make room for the works, and since then the trains have had to reach the main part of
the station by a double reversal — running past and backing in — which added to every journey.
From the timetable change the line will instead be joined directly to platform four, which Správa
železnic opened on 20 October as part of the first finished half of the rebuilt station. That direct
connection, according to Zdopravy.cz, is what takes more than ten minutes off the run.

It is the clearest return yet on a reconstruction that has mostly cost passengers time. When the
rebuilt half of the station opened in October, platform four was described as intended mainly for
trains on the Semmering line towards Zličín and Hostivice. Seven weeks later, that is what it will be
doing.

## The trains and the timetable

The class 810 railcars give way to Stadler RS1 units of class 841.3, which České dráhy markets as
RegioSpider. They are modernised vehicles that originally ran in Germany and which the operator is
gradually putting onto regional lines in Central Bohemia. They are low-floor and air-conditioned, and
offer Wi-Fi and sockets for charging — none of which the 810s have.

S65 is also being strengthened at peak. Selected trains will continue beyond Hostivice to Noutonice
or Středokluky, and two trains an hour will run from Hostivice over the Semmering to Smíchov in the
peaks. The branch through Chýně to Rudná u Prahy keeps its present hourly interval.

## Before then, the closure

For the moment nothing runs over the Semmering at all. Trains between Smíchov and Zličín have been
suspended since 13 August because of the Smíchov modernisation, and the closure is due to end on
30 November. On the rest of the line — between Zličín, Hostivice and Rudná u Prahy, for instance —
the 810s are still in service until the timetable change.

The swap is one step in the class's retreat from regular service with České dráhy. At the end of
September the operator still had 35 of the railcars in its regular diagrams, Zdopravy.cz noted; the
rest are kept as an operational reserve.

## Reading it

The story here is not nostalgia for a boxy railcar. It is that a line running out of the city
centre to Hostivice has spent three years paying for a station rebuild in reversals and closures, and will now be paid back in a direct
platform, a faster run and a denser peak service. The figures come from a single outlet relaying
ROPID's information; the exact new journey times are not given in the report and are not stated
here.
