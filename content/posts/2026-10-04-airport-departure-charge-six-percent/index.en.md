The board of Letiště Praha has voted to raise the charge an airline pays for every passenger
departing from Terminal 1 or Terminal 2 by six per cent. Zdopravy.cz reports the vote as unanimous
and gives the figures precisely: CZK 682 today, CZK 723 after the increase, a rise of 41 crowns. The
new price list is being prepared for the period beginning 28 March 2027. Czech Television, which
broke the decision in an interview with the chairman, put the same change in round numbers — roughly
680 crowns now, about forty crowns more.

Radomír Lašák gave the reason in one line.

> On the board we approved six per cent as one man. We have to react to energy prices.
>
> Radomír Lašák, chairman of the board, Letiště Praha

## Two years of no increase, and a figure for inflation

The rate going up has stood since the end of March 2024, when it rose from CZK 649. The airport's
own case for the increase is that charges have lagged: it says airport fees rose only five per cent
between 2021 and 2024 while their real value fell, and puts cumulative inflation across 2023 to 2025
at 16.2 per cent. On that account this is not a step change but a partial catch-up on costs already
incurred.

There is also something to spend it on. ČT24 reports that construction of a new multi-storey car
park at the airport starts next year, and that this is part of why the management needs money for
further development. The broadcaster notes that airport charges are most often between ten and forty
per cent of a ticket price, depending on how far the flight goes and what aircraft flies it — on its
own example, a return to Rome in mid-April next year costing about CZK 3,600 carries roughly 680
crowns of passenger tax.

## The sequencing question

Lašák told Czech Television the increase first has to be discussed with the airlines. Zdopravy.cz
sets that against the airport's own website and its published invitation, which had the formal
consultation on the draft price list taking place on 16 September — before the interview. The
outlet does not resolve the discrepancy and neither will we; it is the one point on which the
official account and the published timetable do not line up.

Opposition to the rise arrived before the number did. Martin Kupka, chair of ODS and a former
transport minister, said on 24 September that his information from inside the airport was that the
new management was preparing a "sharp increase in airport taxes", and that higher costs would show
up in ticket prices.

Zdopravy.cz also points to the precedent airlines reach for. Ryanair has responded to rising charges
elsewhere by withdrawing capacity: this year it announced the closure of its Thessaloniki base for
the winter season, about 500,000 seats and ten routes, blaming Fraport Greece's fees for being 66
per cent higher than before the pandemic. How Europe's largest carrier reacts at Ruzyně is, as the
outlet puts it, still being established.

## Whose decision this is

Lašák joined the board with effect from 1 August and was elected its chairman on 3 August, replacing
Jiří Pos. His arrival drew criticism from unions and part of the opposition, and in July five union
organisations at the airport declared a strike alert over his selection. Zdopravy.cz reports they
are not yet considering lifting it, because they are still waiting for his plans. The price list for
March 2027 is the first of them to become public.
