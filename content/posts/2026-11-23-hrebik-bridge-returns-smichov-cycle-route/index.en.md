The railway bridge over Nádražní street in Smíchov known as Hřebík — "the Nail" — is due back in
place early in December, more than a year after it was lifted out. Setting the repaired steel
structure on its new supports is scheduled for the night of 7 to 8 December, according to a newly
published traffic order from Prague City Hall reported by Zdopravy.cz on 22 November. It returns
without its rails: the bridge that carried trains for decades will carry people on foot and on
bikes.

## What the bridge was, and why it came down

The steel structure was made in Budapest in 1974. It carried a line into the northern part of
Smíchov station, and that part of the station has since given way to the new Smíchov City
district, so the bridge had gradually lost its purpose. According to the city's road authority,
TSK, it was also in poor technical condition.

Builders lifted it out on 21 July 2025. Its renovation and return to new abutments was planned
from the moment it was removed, Zdopravy.cz reports.

## What it becomes

The refurbished span will sit on a new substructure with deep foundations on micropiles. It gets a
new deck and barriers so that pedestrians and cyclists can use it safely. The project also covers
changes to the adjoining roads, the tram overhead wiring, and a connection to the former railway
embankment — which is what turns a single bridge into a route.

Zdopravy.cz published alongside its report a TSK plan of the cycle connections around the future
Smíchov Terminal, showing how the bridge is meant to link into the network forming around the
station.

## The night of the lift

Installing the span requires a full closure of Nádražní between U Železničního mostu and the
railway bridges towards Výtoň, from 22:00 on 7 December until 4:00 the following morning. Trams
will also stop running; replacement buses are to run between Anděl and Smíchovské nádraží, and
diversions will be signed for cars.

## One piece of a larger rebuild

Hřebík is one of the smaller parts of the reconstruction of Smíchov. Správa železnic is
modernising the station's tracks, platforms and underpasses and building a new link towards
Radlice. The rebuilt third platform and the new fourth opened to passengers in October; the
railway part of the work is due to be finished in 2028, after which construction of the transport
terminal, with a bus station above the tracks, is to follow. New cycle paths and the Smíchov City
district are being built around it at the same time.

Most of what that rebuild has delivered so far has been for trains. This is a small exception, and
an economical one: a structure the railway no longer needed, kept rather than scrapped, and put
back over the same street with a different job.
