## Corrections

Found an error? Send the article URL and the source that contradicts it to
[corrections@pragueinsider.cz](mailto:corrections@pragueinsider.cz). Substantive corrections are
noted in the article itself.

## Publishers

If you publish one of the outlets Prague Insider reads and want your material handled differently,
attributed differently, or excluded from the scan entirely, write to
[editor@pragueinsider.cz](mailto:editor@pragueinsider.cz). Removal requests are honoured without
argument.

## Tips

Know about a project, a hearing or a decision that the specialist press has not picked up? Send it
to [editor@pragueinsider.cz](mailto:editor@pragueinsider.cz). Note that Prague Insider publishes
only what a citable source has already reported — a tip is a pointer for the scan, not something we
can publish on its own.
