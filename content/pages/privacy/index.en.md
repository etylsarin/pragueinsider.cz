Prague Insider is a static website. There is no account system, no comment system, no newsletter
database and no advertising network.

## What we collect

Nothing. The site sets no cookies, runs no analytics script, and embeds no third-party trackers,
social widgets or fonts. Typefaces are served from this domain.

## What our host sees

The site is served by GitHub Pages. Like any web server, GitHub records requests — including your IP
address — in order to deliver pages and defend against abuse. That processing is GitHub's, under
[GitHub's Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement).
We have no access to those logs.

## The map page

The [map](/en/map/) loads background tiles from CARTO, which necessarily reveals your IP address to
CARTO for the tiles you request. No other page loads anything from another domain. If you would
rather not contact CARTO at all, simply do not open the map — every article page works without it.

## Links out

Every article links to its original sources on other websites. Those sites have their own privacy
practices, which we neither control nor endorse.

## Email

If you write to us, we hold your message for as long as it takes to deal with it. We do not add
correspondents to any list.

Questions about this policy: [privacy@pragueinsider.cz](mailto:privacy@pragueinsider.cz).
