Trams stop running between Olšanské náměstí and Nákladové nádraží Žižkov from about 4.30am on
Saturday 29 August until the end of Saturday 10 October. PID gives the reason in one line:
preparatory work before construction of a new tram line. That line is Olšanská–Habrová, and the
closure is the first physical sign that it is actually being built.

## What changes on the network

Five lines are affected, and the pattern is a familiar one — the network folds inward around the
missing section.

- **Line 34 is cancelled** outright.
- **Line 26** is cut back to terminate at Olšanské hřbitovy instead of running through to Nákladové
  nádraží.
- **Lines 9 and 95** are diverted via Flora and Olšanské hřbitovy to Želivského.
- **Line 5** is rerouted entirely, and in two stages.

Line 5 is the one worth following. Over the weekend of 29 and 30 August it runs via Masarykovo
nádraží, Náměstí Republiky and Strossmayerovo náměstí to Nádraží Holešovice, where it continues as
line 6. From Monday 31 August it swaps Strossmayerovo náměstí for Bílá labuť and Těšnov — because
that is the day tram service returns to Hlávkův most. Praha 7's town hall, which put out its own
notice on 24 August, frames the change as a gain rather than a loss for Holešovice: from Vltavská
the 5 gives a faster run into the centre, and line 12 a faster run into Karlín.

PID's notice lists no replacement bus service for the closed section.

## The line the closure is for

DPP awarded the Olšanská–Habrová contract in May. A consortium of FIRESTA, rekonstrukce, stavby;
PEDASTA, dopravní stavby; and N+N – Konstrukce a dopravní stavby Litoměřice won with a bid of CZK
599.2 million excluding VAT — almost CZK 110 million under the tender's estimated value. The build
period is 12 months, and DPP said at the time that construction would begin in the third quarter of
this year. A closure starting on 29 August is that promise landing on schedule.

The route is unusual for Prague. Nearly two kilometres long, it runs mostly in the alignment of the
disused Praha-Žižkov – Praha-Malešice local railway and through the former Nákladové nádraží Žižkov
site, where a new district is being built. DPP reuses the railway formation as far as possible, and
over roughly the last 600 metres reuses the railway track itself, adapted for tram operation.

It starts from a new junction off Jana Želivského at Olšanská and ends in a stub terminus south of
Osiková. About two thirds of it — 1.34 kilometres, from Jana Želivského to just past the future
Vackov stop — is double track. Around a hundred metres beyond Vackov it becomes single track. Power
comes from a new substation DPP is building on Na Viktorce.

## Six stops, now with final names

The stop names were confirmed by Prague City Hall's transport department at the same time as the
contract:

- **Nákladové nádraží Žižkov** and **Bernasova** (working names Nový Žižkov and Nákladová), both on
  a newly laid street inside the former freight yard, serving the district going up there.
- **Třebešín** (working name Malešická), where the alignment passes under K Červenému dvoru. DPP
  says these will markedly improve access for existing housing north and south of the tracks, served
  today only by buses; the nearby Třebešín bus stops will be renamed Zvěřinova once trams run.
- **Vackov** (working name Pod Kapličkou), at the crossing with the footpath linking Nad Kapličkou to
  Malešická.
- **Habrová**, on the single-track section, with a single platform — passengers will always board
  from the north, whichever way the tram is going. It sits about 150 metres from the existing
  Habrová bus stops.
- **Jarov** (working name Sídliště Jarov), the terminus, a two-track stub with an island platform
  south of Osiková.

The project is co-financed by the European Union through the Transport Programme 2021–2027.

Read together, the closure notice and the May contract say the same thing from two directions: the
six weeks of missing trams on Žižkov are the cost of a line that turns a derelict freight railway
into scheduled public transport for a district that does not exist yet.
