The sixteen days that Smíchov's trams are losing come down to two nights of crane work. Správa
železnic has told Zdopravy.cz that the remaining steel for the railway bridge over Nádražní will be
seated in two stages — the night of 6 to 7 October and the night of 8 to 9 October — inside a works
window that opened on Saturday 3 October and closes on Sunday 18 October.

> The steel structures will be brought to the site during the night hours. A crane will then seat
> them one after another on the supports prepared for them.
>
> David Kabele, spokesman, Správa železnic

The works also take in alterations to the tram overhead line, which is part of why the closure is
longer than the lifts.

## How much bridge is left

This is where the two accounts in circulation do not agree, and it is worth setting out rather than
smoothing over. Správa železnic, via Zdopravy.cz, describes the first part as a steel structure
36.5 metres long weighing roughly 170 tonnes, carrying one track and forming **one third** of the
future crossing — which makes the two structures now going in the remaining two thirds. ČT24
describes it as a two-part job instead: the northern portion replaced last year, the southern
section receiving its new steel now. Both outlets agree on the finished state. The bridge over
Nádražní will carry three tracks.

That is the number the whole stretch turns on. Of the three railway bridges between Smíchovské
nádraží and the river, this is the only one being built to three tracks in the current phase. The
structures over Svornosti and Strakonická are going in as two-track bridges, and Zdopravy.cz reports
that the three-track arrangement over Nádražní is sized to match the capacity of the planned railway
bridge over the Vltava. The 1955 structure it replaces is gone.

## What runs while it goes in

Trams do not run between Na Knížecí and Smíchovské nádraží from 3 to 18 October. Lines 12 and 15
terminate at Na Knížecí. Line 20 is sent along Radlická, which Zdopravy.cz describes as running via
Křížová and ČT24 as turning towards Anděl. Lines 5 and 94 are diverted over Dvorecký most, according
to ČT24.

Replacement bus X12 links Anděl, Na Knížecí and Smíchovské nádraží by day, with night bus X90
running Palackého náměstí and Karlovo náměstí to Anděl and Zlíchov. South of the gap, two island
tram routes keep the network together: 32 between Slivenec and Smíchovské nádraží, and 40 between
Smíchovské nádraží and Modřany. PID publishes the detail.

## The bill behind it

The bridge is one line item in the modernisation of Praha-Smíchov station, which Správa železnic
puts at about 5.1 billion crowns. Alongside the track, the work covers rebuilt platforms and
underpasses, an entirely new fourth platform, and a footbridge across the tracks towards Radlice.

Two nights of lifting is a small thing to set against that figure. It is also the thing that decides
how many trains the section can take once Výtoň is settled, which is why a crane over Nádražní in
the small hours is worth more attention than a tram diversion usually gets.
