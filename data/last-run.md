- **`cinovecka-narrows-to-one-lane`** (transport, candidate 18, the lowest-scored item on the list)
  — TSK is replacing the surface on 4.3 km of the Cínovecká inbound carriageway from 23 October to
  10 December, cutting the approach from the D8 to one lane, while the last stage of the Kbelská
  reconstruction runs on the interchange it feeds until 30 November. Written from both Zdopravy.cz
  reports (14 and 9 October) fetched with a browser user-agent; the two are consistent on dates and
  scope, so there is no disagreement to report. The point that makes it more than a roadworks
  notice: Kbelská stands in for the unfinished Pražský okruh and carries all D1/D11/D10/D8 transit,
  so there is no second substitute to divert onto — and Cínovecká narrows on 23 October, the day
  before Kbelská enters a phase whose traffic measures TSK has not yet approved. Pinned on
  Cínovecká in Ďáblice, inside the resurfaced section.
