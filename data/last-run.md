### Written (4)

- **`libus-nove-dvory-tram-construction-start`** (transport) — foundation stone laid 7 October in the
  temporary stub at Libuš. Two sources, cross-checked: DPP's release carries the engineering (1.8 km,
  four stops, 78 construction and operational objects, almost 2 km of DN 1200 Želivka main relocated
  along Novodvorská, phase one to end-2027, trial running end-2028, underpass foundations for the
  future metro D station) and Zdopravy.cz carries what DPP's release omits — the price, CZK 1.26bn
  excl. VAT to OHLA ŽS, and the nine-month slip from a January start while ÚOHS examined T4 Building's
  complaint and found no fault. **One disagreement left unresolved and attributed:** DPP dates the
  preceding Sídliště Modřany – Libuš section to the end of May 2023, Zdopravy calls it "předloni";
  the article gives DPP's explicit date and says it is DPP's. The CZK ~700m/km figure is our division
  of two sourced numbers, with Zdopravy's own explanation (the water mains) attached. Also carries
  Šurovský's verbatim Zdopravy answer on what comes next, which is the one thing here with a horizon
  past this election: the tangential ring, Prague 8–Prague 6 first, Podolí–Michle off Dvorecký most,
  with Suchdol and Motol–Vypich deprioritised and only Zdiby holding a final zoning decision.
- **`opatov-park-and-ride-opens`** (transport) — opened 7 October beside metro C; 495 spaces for CZK
  663.8m excl. VAT (803m with tax), CZK 1.62m a space. Single source (Zdopravy.cz), attributed by
  name, including the "probably the dearest in the country" judgement, which is the outlet's and is
  reported as such. Written because the procurement history is the story and not the ribbon: the 2022
  award to BAK / Metrostav DIZ / Geosan, the Spolu criticism after that autumn's election, ÚOHS
  cancelling the award, the 2024 re-tender, and March 2025 giving it back to the same consortium —
  against České Budějovice's Stromovka at CZK 354,000 a space for the same capacity. The rooftop park
  the premium partly pays for is the part that is not finished, and the piece says so.
- **`liben-soumosti-u-lodenice-tender`** (transport) — TSK has advertised the completion of the
  part-built most U Loděnice (SO205): CZK 197m base, 40m in conditional sums, CZK 237m total excl. VAT.
  Single source (Zdopravy.cz). The best of the day and today's lead: a project the Brno regional court
  froze in May, by upholding an ÚOHS ban on continuing the old contract, is moving again — and moving
  by being broken into pieces, with two further tenders (designer, construction manager) still to come
  before the rest of the soumostí can start. Both the Beránek and Hájek quotes are verbatim. The piece
  is explicit that the tram return is a temporary state preceding the Vltava bridge repair, not a
  substitute for it.
- **`zlata-ulicka-facade-restoration`** (architecture) — restorers have started on the Golden Lane
  facades for over CZK 6.5m, working back to the 1955 Janák/Trnka scheme, from house No. 12 by
  Daliborka upward, in stages that keep the lane open. Single source: the Prague Castle Administration's
  release as reprinted by Praha 1, attributed that way in the frontmatter and the text. Written for the
  exclusion rather than the paint — No. 19 is left out of a cosmetic programme because its structure
  needs a project of its own, and neither that project's cost nor this one's completion date is given.
  An earlier draft carried a cost for the 2010s reconstruction of the lane taken from a web search
  rather than a source; it was cut.
