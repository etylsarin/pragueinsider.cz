  group on 5 October to write the brief for an architectural and artistic competition for a
  permanent memorial on náměstí Jana Palacha to the fourteen people killed at the Faculty of Arts
  on 21 December 2023. Named membership (UK rectorate, FF UK, Spojení nadějí, IPR, magistrát,
  GHMP), bereaved families and survivors to take part, and three live constraints: the temporary
  memorial already in the square, the square's own pending reconstruction, and a competition the

  announcement). The city's own release on praha.eu could not be fetched — see notes.
- **`ladvi-zabak-fountain-rebuilt`** (public-space) — the rebuilt water feature by koupaliště Ládví
  on sídliště Ďáblice passed its first load test on 8 October and water runs under the Žabák again
  after decades. A faithful replica of Vincenc Vingler's bronze, cast from a model supplied by the
  sculptor's family, replaces the original that vandals tore off in 2007 and thieves took for good
  in 2008; the piece stays at the Praha 8 town hall until the security cameras are mounted. Two
  verbatim quotes (Hřebík, Solomonová). Single source (Praha 8). Noted in the article that the
  district's headline says twenty years while its own text dates the final theft to 2008 — reported,
  not resolved.
