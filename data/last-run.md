### Written to the queue (2)

- **petrin-funicular-stations-rebuild-schedule** — The funicular reopened on Tuesday; its three
  stations were not part of that reconstruction, and Prague now has a sequence for them. First
  deputy mayor for transport Jaromír Beránek told Zdopravy.cz that talks on the final arrangement
  finish this year, project preparation runs through 2027 (possibly with the first stage at Újezd
  inside it), Nebozízek and the upper Petřín station follow in 2028, and a second Újezd stage
  closes it. The decision behind the schedule is the city council's June resolution: all three
  stations step-free, 80 million crowns excluding VAT, completion 2030, two lifts at Újezd, one at
  Nebozízek, a new side entrance at Petřín from the Rose Garden. Distinct from yesterday's
  published reopening piece — that one recorded the 2030 date, this one carries the phasing, the
  cost, the lift counts and the property problem at the lower station. Single source, attributed
  by name in the text; fetched with a browser user-agent, since Zdopravy 403s WebFetch.
- **pid-litacka-second-generation-funding** — Prague's councillors approved 25 million crowns for a
  second generation of the PID Lítačka app, with six million more from ROPID and IDSK. Money
  committed, a named decision, a stated replacement date (an ordinary update in early 2027), and a
  scale worth recording: ~200,000 unique users a day, more than 109 million journey searches in
  2025. Borderline on remit — an app is not the built environment — but a step-free journey planner
  that accounts for working lifts is accessibility infrastructure, and the piece says so plainly
  rather than pretending otherwise. Citywide, so no `district` and no `location`. Single source
  (Zdopravy.cz), attributed by name.
