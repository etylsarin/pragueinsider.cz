**Written (2), both released today — queue was empty at the start and is empty again.**

- **Published, lead** — `svatopluka-cecha-parking-split` (public-space). Praha 10 put out the
  record of the second public hearing on ra15's competition-winning design for náměstí Svatopluka
  Čecha. Two hard numbers: the scheme cuts parking by about 15 %, and just over a third of the room
  — six of them living on the square — asked for more. Contested, specific, and the pressure runs
  the opposite way from the usual consultation. Sources: the district page and the full meeting
  record (PDF) by Architekti prostoru.
- **Published** — `nadrazni-railway-bridge-second-half` (transport). Nádražní closed 2–19 October
  and five tram lines suspended 3–18 October for the erection of a steel railway bridge structure.
  Not filed as a diversion notice: on the sequence published by Praha 5 (Jan 2025) and Zdopravy.cz
  (Jul 2025, Aug 2026) this is the second half of the bridge over Nádražní, the two-track one that
  makes it the only three-track span of the three between Smíchov and Výtoň. The inference about
  *which* half is attributed in the text as a reading of the published sequence; the district notice
  itself does not say.
