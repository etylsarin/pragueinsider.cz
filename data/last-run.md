### Written to the queue (2)

- **Dětem v Praze 6 se dnes otevřela nová ZŠ Na Kocínce a MŠ Libocká** (archiweb/ČTK) →
  `praha-6-kocince-libocka-schools-open`. Two school buildings opened on the first day of term:
  a passive-standard pavilion of ZŠ Bílá on Na Kocínce (240 places, CZK ~150m, CZK 100m from the
  city plus SFŽP support, AND atelier — Jaromír Kosnar and Radovan Kupka) and the rebuilt
  MŠ Libocká (110 places, CZK ~126m, CZK 18m from the city). Something opened, with budgets, named
  architects and named officials. Pinned at Na Kocínce.
- **Praha 7 vítá školáky barevnějšími ulicemi** (MČ Praha 7) → `praha-7-asphalt-art-school-streets`.
  Rajská, Plynární and Jablonského repainted over July and August under the district's asfalt art
  programme, Antonínská to follow in September; designs by pupils of the local schools, artist
  Jakub Karlík. Delivered, physical, and reads against the Strossmayerovo bollards we covered on
  31 August. Written with the safety claim attributed to the district rather than asserted.
